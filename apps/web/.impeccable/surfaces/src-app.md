# Application surfaces

Mode: Operate. Scope: T119–T140, access, company/team, warehouses, catalogue and
inventory list. The user chose a familiar light SaaS interface with blue accent,
code-first, and delegated craft references while keeping Radix/Tailwind.
Craft references: Linear's compact hierarchy and GitHub's settings/forms. These
are quality references, not claims of using their design systems. MCP references
48, 45 and 29 were inspected; their marketing compositions do not supply WMS controls.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a familiar operating surface where company, warehouse and unit remain
obvious. No decorative dashboard metrics or simulated 3D before their tasks.

OWN-WORLD: white work area, cool pale neutral rail, blue current selection and
primary actions, Manrope sans, tabular quantities, 8 px controls and 12 px dialogs.
Semantic red/amber/green always accompany text. Density is compact, not crowded.

STORY: identify company and warehouse, find a product or location, read exact
physical/reserved/available quantities, and perform only authorized administration.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 224 px left navigation, 64 px context bar, 32 px title and concise
description, filters immediately above a semantic table. Mobile has a top menu,
full-width context selector and stacked labeled rows. Access is a focused form.
Signature: changing warehouse updates the scope and its inventory together,
preserving the same three-quantity reading order in desktop and mobile.

FORM: category canon, explicitly chosen by the user. Seed 1b34d2d5. The assigned
candidate 6 and creative challengers were not adopted; familiarity is the commitment.
Occasional dialogs use 180 ms opacity/scale feedback; keyboard/reduced motion is
instant. Data and routine page navigation do not animate.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Agreed scope and proof

Forms retain drafts after rejection. Errors are next to fields; uncertainty keeps
the operation reference and explicit recovery actions. No optimistic stock, offline
queue, unavailable UI modules, commercial claims or external runtime dependencies.
Use the real synthetic seed; label demo mode. Playwright covers desktop/mobile,
keyboard, empty/loading/error/permission and two-session changes. No approved comp
exists: this is code-led, not a fidelity claim against an image.
